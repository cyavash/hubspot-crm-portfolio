04 / Commercial details & next steps

Everything needed to move forward.

Scope, term and billing

The proposed configuration covers three locations and eight additional provider seats for a 12-month term. Implementation is billed once. Recurring charges follow the selected billing schedule. Changes to locations, seats or selected services require an updated quote.

Data preparation and responsibilities

The import service covers one agreed CSV file, up to 2,500 records, and one validation pass. This preview uses synthetic data only. Source-system remediation, custom integrations and ongoing data entry are outside this configuration.

Privacy and service documentation

Any required services agreement, data processing terms or business associate agreement must be reviewed separately before production data is transferred. This proposal does not replace those documents.

Validity and changes

This proposal is valid through November 14, 2026. Dates, service details and renewal terms must be confirmed before a production agreement is issued. Optional services are included only when selected in the final quote.
